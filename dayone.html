04THE PIVOT

Halfway through, the research told us we were solving the wrong problem.

Our first instinct was loneliness. The “Silent Researcher” persona showed the real barrier: not knowing how the group works, and being afraid to reveal it.

06DESIGN DECISIONS

Every screen came down to one principle: lower the risk of being seen.

123
  1. 1
    A private panel, not a channel

    Only the newcomer sees it, so nobody knows what they read.

  2. 2
    Patterns, not people

    Topics by share of conversation. Never who posts most.

  3. 3
    Functional language

    We never say “anxiety” in the UI. Naming the fear makes the user feel watched.

123
  1. 1
    Starters in the group’s tone

    Casual, neutral or formal, so the first message fits how this group talks.

  2. 2
    A draft, not a script

    Editable, so saying hello isn’t a decision made alone.

  3. 3
    Nothing sends on its own

    Help sits right above the composer. The newcomer always presses send.

07REFLECTION

The best decision we made was letting the research change our mind.

  • The barrier to belonging is information, not personality.
  • “Anxiety” belongs in the research, never on the screen the anxious person is reading.
  • A privacy-first data model became the reason people would trust the product.

Weeks of silent observation, shrunk to a first day.
