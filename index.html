DAY ONE
IxD Capstone
A chat platform plugin for the first day in a group you don't know yet. Newcomers stay silent for weeks because nobody tells them the unspoken rules, not because they lack the skill. Day One reads the group's own history, turns it into a short private guide, and drafts a first message so saying hello isn't a decision made alone.
REFLOW
Visual Interaction Design 2
A personal planner for days with no room in them. You lay out the day, one thing takes longer than expected, and everything after it is now wrong. reflow catches that moment while it's still happening and puts the rest of the day back together in one tap.
KINDLE
Cinematic Interface
A reader for art and design books on the television. Most reading apps render them as a column of text with the pictures shrunk to fit between paragraphs. Kindle TV gives the image the whole screen, and turns your phone into a loupe you aim at one spot inside it.
MANICPANIC
Packaging Design
A rebranding and packaging project for Manic Panic. Built on a new logo and a restrained, black-on-black palette, it returns to punk's core value, giving courage and a stage to voices outside the mainstream.
SUMMER PALM TREE
