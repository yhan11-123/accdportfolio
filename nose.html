Younhee Han

A website that asks whether you have ever really seen your own face. Long passages of text frame a face with its nose cut out, a gallery of noses tests whether you can recognize your own, and a counter keeps adding up every second you have spent not looking. Shown in the group exhibition “Cool Website, Thanks” at ArtCenter.
